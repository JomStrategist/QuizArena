AI Detective Rule

Not AI: A fixed rule or programmed instruction produces a predictable response — “If X happens, do Y.”

AI: The system uses data, patterns or learned capabilities to recognize, predict, recommend, understand or generate something.

Remember: Automatic does not automatically mean AI.
